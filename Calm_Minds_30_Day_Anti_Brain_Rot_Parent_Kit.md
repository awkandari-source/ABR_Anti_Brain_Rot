# The 30-Day Anti-Brain Rot Screen Reset & Printable Nature Storybook Guide
**Created by ABR Anti-Brain Rot | Evidence-Based Calm Media for Ages 3–6**

## Part 1: The 7-Day Dopamine & Orienting-Reflex Reset Protocol
1. **Replace Fast Cuts with 20-Second Scenes:** Switch from 2-second-cut cartoons to slow watercolor stories (*The Gentle Journey of a Tiny Seed*, *The Sleepy Little Cloud*, *The Little Acorn's Cozy Autumn*).
2. **Enforce the +20 dB Voice Rule:** Never play background music louder than spoken narration during early language windows.
3. **Use Dialogic "Serve & Return" Questions:** Pause once per story and ask your child to point out one color, animal, or sound.
4. **Zero Screens 60 Minutes Before Sleep:** Transition from a 5-minute calm watercolor story to a physical picture book or quiet drawing.

## Part 2: 15 Off-Screen "Soft Fascination" Nature Activities (Ages 3–6)
- **Day 1–3 (The Tiny Seed):** Plant a dry pinto or lima bean in a clear glass jar with a damp paper towel so your child can watch the real white root sprout over 5 days.
- **Day 4–6 (The Sleepy Cloud):** Lie on a blanket outside for 5 minutes and count three slow-moving clouds without speaking.
- **Day 7–9 (The Little Acorn):** Collect 5 fallen leaves and 3 acorns; trace their outlines on warm cream paper with beeswax crayons.
- **Day 10–12 (Woodland Listening):** Close eyes for 60 seconds and count every distinct bird song or wind rustle.
- **Day 13–15 (Breathing Together):** Practice the 4-second "Smell the Flower, Blow the Dandelion" breathing exercise before bedtime.
