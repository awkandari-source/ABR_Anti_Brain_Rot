## Sanitized Continuous Narration Script

Inside a quiet sandstone corridor at the University of Queensland in Australia sits a glass bell jar protecting the longest-running laboratory experiment on Earth. In nineteen twenty-seven, physicist Thomas Parnell warmed a sample of black coal-tar pitch, poured it into a sealed glass funnel, and waited three full years just for the surface to settle before snipping the stem open in nineteen thirty. At room temperature, solid-looking pitch shatters under a hammer, yet gravity pulls it downward as a liquid roughly two hundred and thirty billion times thicker than water. Across nearly a full century, only nine heavy black drops have fallen into the beaker below, each taking roughly a decade to form, reminding us that physics moves patiently even when nothing seems to change.
